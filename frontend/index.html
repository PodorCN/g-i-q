        Methodology & scoring rubric

        Axes & horizons

        x = g − g* and y = π − π*, where g is expected real GDP growth over the next 6–12 months, g* is trend growth (%), π is expected core PCE inflation and π* is the Fed's target (%). Units are percentage points (pp).

        House scoring & staleness

        Each institution's latest commentary is scored with a fixed rubric: explicit forecasts convert directly (CPI forecasts minus 0.4pp to put them on a PCE basis); qualitative views map to fixed anchors. 1σ uncertainty is 0.3pp for point forecasts, 0.5pp for ranges, 0.8pp for qualitative views (+0.2pp for tail risks). Views older than days are marked stale (hollow, dashed) and weighted ×.

        Hard-data model & blend

        Each FRED series maps to an implied gap via a documented heuristic (Okun's law, CFNAI × 1.5, CPI − 0.4pp). The model point is the weighted mean and dispersion plus a pp floor. "Blended" combines all house views with the hard-data model ( weight), using Monte Carlo draws and bivariate normal √χ²₂(p) confidence regions.

        · Data: FRED (St. Louis Fed) and public commentary from each institution. Not investment advice.
